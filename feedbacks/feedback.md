## Themes (what keeps coming up)

- **Sharing & collaboration** — Users want to share plans with travel partners, not just view solo.
- **Multi-destination support** — Multiple respondents hit limitations with multi-city or multi-stop trips.
- **Calendar integration** —  Users expect syncing with external calendars (Google, Outlook).
- **Performance** — Longer itineraries cause noticeable lag; optimization needed.
- **Destination coverage** — Popular destinations work well, but niche locations return empty results.

## Top 3 things to fix

- [ ] Add itinerary sharing (export to PDF, shareable link, or invite collaborators)
- [ ] Support multi-city / multi-destination trip planning
- [ ] Fix performance issues with long itineraries (10+ days)
